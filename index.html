How we use your details: Privacy note

Good to know

Questions, answered

Anything else? Message us and we'll get back to you.

How many guests can stay?

The suite sleeps 2 adults, with 1 bedroom, 1 double bed and 1 bathroom. If you're bringing little ones and don't mind a cozy squeeze, we can add a crib or a bed for young children. Children are by arrangement, so please talk to us before you book.

What are the check-in and check-out times?

Check-in is after 3pm and check-out is before 10am. Check-in is self check-in with a lockbox, so you can arrive on your own schedule within those times.

Are pets allowed?

Yes, pets are welcome, with a maximum of 1 pet.

Is there parking?

Yes. There is free on-site parking with EV charging.

Is it within walking distance of shuls and shops?

Yes. The suite is within walking distance of local shuls and shops, and close to bus lines.

Is the availability form an instant booking?

No. The form is an inquiry, not an instant reservation. We confirm dates and prices with you directly. If you'd like to book instantly, you can book on Airbnb.
